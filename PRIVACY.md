# Duey Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 5, 2026

Duey is a deadline helper that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it handles, where that data goes, and what you control. Duey is not affiliated with any school or learning-platform company.

## The short version
- Duey keeps your deadlines and settings on your computer. There is no Duey account, no Duey server, no advertising and no analytics.
- The people who make or share Duey do not receive your data.
- A few optional features send limited data to other companies. Each one is listed below and only applies if you turn it on.

## What Duey stores on your computer
Duey saves its files in a folder named .duey in your user folder (on Windows, C:\Users\YourName\.duey). It holds:
- Your settings: portal address, calendar link, security key, email address, any mail or API keys you add, and the browser extension pairing code.
- Your deadlines: course names, titles, due dates, links, assignment descriptions, whether an item is done, changes Duey noticed, reminders, study help results and focus-timer minutes.

These files are not encrypted. Where your operating system supports it, Duey limits the settings file to your user account, but anyone who can use your account or copy your files can read them. Duey never saves your school password. If you use the optional sign-in, the password is used once to ask the portal for a key and is then discarded; the key is saved.

## Who Duey talks to
- Your school portal. To read your deadlines, Duey sends your calendar link or security key to your portal's address, the way a web browser would. Your school's systems may log these requests, and your school's own rules and privacy policy apply to that data.
- Google Fonts. Duey's page and the extension popup load fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com), so Google can see your IP address and browser details. If the fonts are blocked, Duey falls back to your system fonts.
- Email, if you enter an email address. Duey sends alert emails through a mail server. By default that is a sending account set up by whoever runs your copy of Duey; if you use the Advanced option, it is your own mail account. Emails contain course names, assignment titles, due dates and short reminder text. The mail provider, and the owner of the sending account, can see the recipient address and the contents.
- YouTube, if you add a YouTube key. Duey sends search words taken from assignment titles and course names, plus your key, to Google's YouTube service. Video thumbnails load from YouTube in your browser. Without a key, Study help only shows ordinary YouTube search links, which contact YouTube when you click them.
- Anthropic, if you add a Claude key. When you open Study help, Duey sends the course name, assignment title, due date and up to the first 5,000 characters of the assignment description to Anthropic using your key. The result is saved on your computer. Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy cover what happens to that text.

## The browser extension
The extension reads your portal while you are logged in, using the login your browser already has. It only reads the site you approve, and only the list of your upcoming deadlines. It hands that list to Duey at 127.0.0.1, which is your own computer, and a pairing code stops other programs and websites from sending data to Duey.
- It stores the portal address, the pairing code and the last deadline list in your browser's extension storage.
- It asks for access to the single site you approve, to your own computer, and to the storage, alarms and active tab features of your browser.
- It does not read your password, other tabs, or your browsing history, and it sends nothing to the people who made it.
- Choosing Disconnect in the extension, or removing the extension, deletes what it stored.

## What Duey never does
- It does not sell, share or analyze your data for advertising.
- It does not send usage statistics or crash reports to anyone.
- It does not contact any server run by the people who made Duey.

## Your choices
- To delete everything Duey knows, close Duey and delete the .duey folder.
- To turn email off, clear the email address in settings.
- To remove a saved key or link, delete the .duey folder, or ask whoever set up Duey to help you edit the settings file.
- To stop the extension reading your portal, choose Disconnect in the extension popup.
- Because the people who make Duey hold none of your data, requests to see or delete your data are handled by you, on your own computer.

## School rules
Using Duey with your school account is your choice. Check that your school's rules allow automated or third-party tools, and keep your calendar link, security key and pairing code private, since they give access to your deadlines.
