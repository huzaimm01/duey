# Duey browser extension

Reads your deadlines from your course portal (while you're logged in) and hands them to the
Duey app on this computer. It never sees your password and sends nothing anywhere else.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

## Install (about a minute)
1. Start Duey (`python duey.py`), open **Settings**, choose **Browser extension**, and note the pairing code.
2. In your browser, go to `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this `duey-extension` folder.
5. Pin Duey from the puzzle-piece menu so the icon stays visible.
6. Open your course portal, log in, click the Duey icon, type the pairing code and press **Connect**.

Keep your browser open and Duey stays up to date. The icon badge shows how many things are overdue or due within two days.
